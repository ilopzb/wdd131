Site Name

Tender Mercies Thoughts for quiet hearts

I chose the name Tender Mercies because the site focuses on recognizing the small and meaningful ways God shows His love, guidance, and presence in everyday life. The name reflects the personal and spiritual nature of the stories and reflections shared throughout the site.

Tagline: Thoughts for quiet hearts.

Site Purpose

Tender Mercies is a personal faith-based blog that shares reflections, personal experiences, scripture insights, and stories about faith, Jesus Christ, family, growth, repentance, hope, and recognizing God's hand throughout life's journey.

The site is intended to provide a quiet and uplifting place where visitors can pause, read meaningful reflections, and find encouragement through personal experiences and faith-centered messages.

Visitors can browse published reflections, learn more about the purpose of the blog, contact the author, and subscribe to receive future reflections.

Scenarios

  • How can I better recognize God's hand and tender mercies in my everyday life?
  • Where can I find uplifting reflections that can strengthen my faith during difficult or uncertain times?
  • Where can I read previous reflections about faith, family, repentance, hope, and personal growth?
  • How can I stay connected and receive future reflections?

Typography

Georgia / Times New Roman

Georgia, with Times New Roman as a fallback, is used for the main body text, headings, reflection titles, and quotes. The serif style gives Tender Mercies a thoughtful, personal, and literary appearance.

Arial / Helvetica

Arial, with Helvetica as a fallback, is used for navigation, buttons, labels, categories, metadata, and other interface text. The sans-serif style provides a clean contrast and improves readability.
