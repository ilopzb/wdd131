The Heart Behind These Pages

Whether you arrive with questions, gratitude, or a weary heart, I'm grateful you're here.

These pages are a collection of reflections on faith, life, family, books, and the moments that quietly shape us. Some were born from joyful experiences, while others came from seasons of waiting, uncertainty, or growth.

My hope is that wherever you are in your journey, you'll find something here that encourages you to pause, reflect, and recognize God's hand in your own life.

A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Israel. I have always believed that some of life's greatest lessons are found in quiet moments—not necessarily during life's biggest milestones, but in the ordinary days when God gently reminds us that He is near.

Writing began as a simple desire to preserve those moments, not because I have all the answers, but because I know what it feels like to need hope, direction, and peace.

Over time, I realized that many of the experiences that strengthened my faith were not dramatic miracles, but quiet reminders that the Lord had been walking beside me all along.

The Story Behind the Name

The name Tender Mercies comes from one of my favorite scriptural expressions: the tender mercies of the Lord.

To me, tender mercies are the quiet evidence of God's care, love, and presence. They are often found in ordinary moments and sometimes recognized only when we pause long enough to look back and see His hand in our lives.

Whether a season of life is filled with joy, uncertainty, growth, or sorrow, I believe God continues to work through small and simple things.
